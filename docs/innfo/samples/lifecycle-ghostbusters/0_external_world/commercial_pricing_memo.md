# Ghostbusters Commercial Pricing & Service Strategy

## Manhattan Commercial Rates
Standard paranormal elimination rates are set at $5,000 per Class IV entity capture, plus an ongoing $1,000 monthly containment storage fee per ectoplasmic entity.

## Hazardous Entanglement Surcharge
Entities exhibiting physical slime projection or localized PK turbulence incur an additional $1,500 hazard charge.
