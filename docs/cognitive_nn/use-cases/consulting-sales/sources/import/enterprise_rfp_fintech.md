# Global Bank RFP: Enterprise Cloud Modernization & Knowledge Audit

Section 3.1: Scope of Work
The vendor must provide enterprise architecture governance, cloud migration support, and automated documentation reconciliation. All deliverables must comply with ISO-27001 and SOC2 Type II standards.

Section 4.2: Rate Card & Team Composition
Responses must provide hourly billing rates for Principal Architect, Senior DevOps Engineer, and Cloud Security Specialist. Bids with unverified subcontractor rates or inconsistent billing tables will be disqualified.

Section 5.3: SLA & Response Turnaround
Proposals must be delivered within 5 business days of RFP issuance.
