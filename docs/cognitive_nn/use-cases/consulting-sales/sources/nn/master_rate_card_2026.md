# Corporate Consulting Master Rate Card (FY2026)

## Standard Hourly Rates & Role Tiers

### Principal Architect Standard Rate
Approved corporate billing rate is `$220.00 / hr`. Required minimum experience: 12+ years and lead certification.

### Senior Cloud Security Specialist Rate
Approved corporate billing rate is `$195.00 / hr`. Required minimum experience: 8+ years and ISO/CISSP credentials.

### Senior DevOps Engineer Rate
Approved corporate billing rate is `$175.00 / hr`. Required minimum experience: 6+ years with CKA/Terraform.

### Blended Delivery Pod Discount Rule
Multi-disciplinary pods of 4+ consultants qualify for a 5% blended rate adjustment to `$185.00 / hr`.
