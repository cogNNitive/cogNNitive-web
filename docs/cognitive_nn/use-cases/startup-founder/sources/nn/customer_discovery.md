# Customer Discovery & Investor Transcripts

## Prospect Feedback: Engineering CTO

Alex (CTO at FinTech scale-up) validated core product-market fit requirements during technical discovery.

### Enterprise Budget Cap & Pricing
Alex confirmed: "If an AI agent can read our real files from Git without sending data to third-party databases, we would pay $49/seat/month without blinking. The budget cap for departmental approval without VP sign-off is $500/month."

### Multi-Tool Fragmentation Friction
Engineering squads are currently fragmented across Confluence, Notion, and Google Docs, leading to stale architecture specifications within 2 weeks.

## Investor Strategy Session

Sarah (Partner at EarlyVentures) identified core defensibility vectors during seed diligence.

### Defensibility & Value Proposition
Sarah stated: "Every AI startup claims to manage knowledge. Your defensibility is mathematical traceability and local-first ownership. If you can prove that non-technical founders can update strategic models in 1 hour instead of 2 weeks, you have a real product."

### Developer Workflow Requirements
Developer teams demand zero-lockin plain Markdown files editable directly in VS Code or Obsidian, coupled with zero-install visual graph rendering for management.
